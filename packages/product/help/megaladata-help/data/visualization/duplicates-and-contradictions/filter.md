# ![](./../../images/icons/toolbar-controls_18x18/toolbar-controls_18x18_filter_default.svg)&nbsp;Filter

The **Filter** command in the visualizer toolbar filters records that match one or more conditions. Multiple conditions can be combined into one complex filter using the AND/OR logical connectors.

## Configuration window

Click the **Filter** button on the toolbar to open the configuration window. Click ![](./images/add_default.svg)&nbsp;**Add filter** to start, then pick a field and a [condition](../../processors/transformation/row-filter/filter-conditions.md) from the dropdown menus. You can combine multiple filters using AND or OR. The system applies AND by default, but you can change this to OR by clicking the **Add OR operator** button next to the add icon.

When done through the settings, click the **Filter** button at the bottom of the window.
